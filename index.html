Opera of the Forsaken

What it is
An experimental exhibition of seven musical instruments, built in ShapeXR as a walkable circular gallery under a white colonnade.
The idea
Instruments are usually shown behind glass with a label. Here, you walk a red carpet that spirals through the columns, and every instrument gets its own alcove, its own story screen and its own mood. The building is the narrator.
My role
3D design, spatial layout, interface screens, visual storytelling.
Experience it
Open it on a screen, or put on a VR headset and walk through it at full scale.
Tool
ShapeXR
Best in VR. This exhibition was designed to be walked through in a headset. Ask me for a demo.
